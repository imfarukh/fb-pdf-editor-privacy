FB PDF Editor – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026
Publisher: Farrukh Bhatti

Summary

FB PDF Editor is an offline PDF editor. Your documents are opened, edited and saved only on your own computer. We do not collect, store or sell your personal data or the contents of your files.

Documents and files

All PDF processing (editing, OCR, conversion, forms, encryption) happens locally on your device. Files are never uploaded to our servers or to third parties.

Internet use

The app works without an internet connection. It connects to the internet only when you ask it to:

  • Additional OCR languages: language data files are downloaded from the public jsDelivr content network (cdn.jsdelivr.net). Only the requested language file name is sent.
  • Create PDF from a web page: the app loads the web address you enter.
  • Links you click open in your web browser.

Purchases and licensing

FB PDF Editor is sold through Microsoft Store. Payments and licenses are handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. The app asks Windows whether your account has a valid license; we do not receive your payment details.

Settings stored on your device

Preferences such as theme, recent files and ribbon layout are stored locally in your Windows user profile and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
